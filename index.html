About Us

Founded in 2019, LearnLoop began with a simple question: why do so many online classrooms leave students behind? Today our platform supports schools, TAFEs and universities with tools such as screen-reader-friendly lesson viewers, captioned video lessons and flexible quizzes that adapt to each learner. Our team of 40 works in a hybrid model, with a studio in Melbourne's CBD and remote roles across Australia.

Why Work With Us

  • Accessibility first: We build to WCAG 2.2 standards and test with real users.
  • Inclusive culture: Flexible hours, remote options and a team that values different perspectives.
  • Grow your skills: Mentoring, a learning budget and time set aside for experimenting.
  • Real impact: Your work is used in classrooms every day.
