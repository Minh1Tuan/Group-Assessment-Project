Learning that includes everyone.

Build the future of accessible online education with us.

LearnLoop is a Melbourne-based EdTech company creating digital learning tools that work for every student, whatever their ability, device or location. We're looking for web developers and designers who care about inclusive design and want their work to make learning easier for thousands of students across Australia and beyond.

Why Work With Us

  • Accessibility first: We build to WCAG 2.2 standards and test with real users.
  • Inclusive culture: Flexible hours, remote options and a team that values different perspectives.
  • Grow your skills: Mentoring, a learning budget and time set aside for experimenting.
  • Real impact: Your work is used in classrooms every day.

Acknowledgement of Country

LearnLoop acknowledges the Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung and Bunurong peoples of the Eastern Kulin Nation, the Traditional Custodians of the land on which our Melbourne studio stands. We pay our respects to Elders past and present, and to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples who contribute to our work and communities.

What this means for us

An acknowledgement is only meaningful if it shapes what we do. At LearnLoop, we are committed to:

  • Welcoming applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples for every role.
  • Designing learning tools that are accessible and inclusive for First Nations learners.
  • Listening to and learning from First Nations voices as we grow as a company.
